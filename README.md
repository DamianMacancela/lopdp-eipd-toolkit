lopdp-eipd-toolkit
==================

*Markdown templates for Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIA/EIPD)*

A repository of templates to document privacy risks under the framework of the Ecuadorian LOPDP and ISO/IEC 29134. Designed for security engineers and IT managers who need a structured, version-controllable (Markdown) way to document risk without relying on heavy word processors.

### Contents
* `plantilla_EIPD.md`: A comprehensive template covering data lifecycle, risk evaluation, and control implementation.
* Risk Matrices (Probability vs. Impact).
* Data mapping workflows.

### Usage
Copy `docs/plantilla_EIPD.md`, fill in the specific use-case data (following the embedded comments), and export to PDF via Pandoc or your preferred Markdown editor.

### Disclaimer
These templates are based on technical interpretations of privacy frameworks and do not constitute official legal advice.
